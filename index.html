Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers.

How do I recharge my balance?
You can recharge your balance by visiting the management office or using our online payment gateway (coming soon).
Can I cancel an order?
Orders can be cancelled within 10 minutes of placing them, provided they haven't been prepared yet.
What happens if I have dietary restrictions?
You can add special instructions during checkout, and our kitchen staff will do their best to accommodate them.
