# UniMeal - Culinary Excellence & Meal Management

## Project Overview
UniMeal is a professional-level web application designed to solve the daily meal management challenges faced by students living in dormitories. Often, students in hostels or dorms lack access to quality home-cooked meals and are forced to rely on expensive or unhealthy outside food. UniMeal provides a centralized platform for students to browse menus, book meals in advance, manage their food budget (balance), and track their order history.

## Why UniMeal?
- **Dormitory Convenience**: Tailored for students who need a reliable and structured way to manage their daily nutrition.
- **Budget Control**: Real-time balance tracking helps students manage their expenses.
- **Advanced Booking**: Prevents food waste and ensures students get their preferred meals.

## Features
- **User Authentication**: Secure login and registration with JWT.
- **Dynamic Menu**: Real-time menu display with descriptions and pricing.
- **Order Management**: Cart system and transaction support.
- **Balance Management**: Initial balance for testing and simulated recharge.
- **Dark/Light Mode**: Premium UI with smooth transitions and glassmorphism.
- **Responsive Design**: Accessible on mobile and desktop.

## Technologies Used
- **Frontend**: HTML5, Vanilla JavaScript, Tailwind CSS, SweetAlert2, Font Awesome.
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express.js, JWT for auth, bcrypt for password hashing.
- **Database**: SQLite (Self-contained for local deployment).
